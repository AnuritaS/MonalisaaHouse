What cookies are

Cookies are small files that websites may place on your device to remember preferences, support functionality, or understand browsing activity. Related technologies may provide similar functions.

What this site currently uses

The downloadable version of this website does not include an advertising pixel, analytics script, cookie banner, or first-party tracking cookie. Visiting linked platforms such as TikTok may involve their cookies.

If services are added

Should we add embedded media, analytics, shopping, booking tools, or other integrations, they may introduce additional cookies or require consent depending on applicable law. We will update this notice and add appropriate controls before enabling relevant tracking.

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you delete or block cookies through settings. Blocking some cookies can affect how third-party pages function. You can also decline to follow external links.
