Cultural diplomacy

Culture can create the conversations that policy continues.

Monalisaa House uses fashion, jewelry, craft, and cultural programming as entry points for conversations about women's economic participation, worker dignity, ethical production, animal welfare, entrepreneurship, and U.S.–South Asia exchange.

We do not claim to perform diplomacy. We create considered spaces where people from different communities, institutions, and markets come together.

Our story

Some stories are stitched across generations.

Long before our first pop-up, there were fabric swatches spread across tables, the quiet music of a workshop, and a teacher’s belief that beautiful things carry the hands and histories of those who make them.

Rooted in a retired fashion professor’s love of Indian artistry, our family’s journey has included 20 years in the export business and more than a decade of fashion shows. In 2025, we began hosting pop-ups-bringing those years of experience out of showrooms and onto shared floors, where a piece of jewellery can spark a memory and a conversation can become a connection.

Today, The Monalisaa House is our way of welcoming you into that story: an intimate meeting place for heritage, discovery, and the joy of finding something made with meaning.

Saumya Srivastava
