Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

At The Monalisaa House, we respect your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you browse this website or reach out to us. Please review and tailor it to your actual operations before publishing.

Information you share

If you email us or use the contact-page email composer, you may share your name, email address, message, and details about your inquiry. The contact form on this static website does not collect or store form entries on our server; it opens your email application so you can choose whether to send the message.

How we use information

We use information you send to reply to inquiries, discuss events and partnerships, and maintain correspondence relevant to your request. We do not sell personal information.

Third-party services

Our pages may load fonts from Google Fonts and placeholder images from Unsplash, which can receive technical information such as your IP address. Following links to outside websites, including social platforms, subjects you to those sites' privacy practices.

Storage and retention

Messages you email to us are handled through our email provider and retained only as reasonably needed for correspondence, legitimate business records, or legal requirements. Ask us if you want us to review or delete a prior message, subject to applicable law.

Your choices

You can avoid sending personal information, choose not to follow external links, and adjust browser settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of certain personal data.

Updates and contact

We may revise this policy as our website changes. For privacy questions, contact monalisaexports@gmail.com (confirm this address before publication).
